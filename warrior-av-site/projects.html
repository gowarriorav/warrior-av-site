Dedicated Theater Build

A basement-to-theater conversion featuring a 120" screen, 4K projector, and 7.1 surround sound. Every component was chosen for performance and hidden from view.

  • Acoustic treatments for optimal audio

Whole-Home Audio System

A six-zone audio install spanning the kitchen, living room, master suite, office, patio, and pool area. Each zone independently controlled from phone or wall panel.

  • Clean install with minimal wall disruption
